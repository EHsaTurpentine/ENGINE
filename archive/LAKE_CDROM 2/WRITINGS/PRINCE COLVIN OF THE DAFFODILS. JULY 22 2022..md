Everyone else gets a break from him. I never have and never will. I have to take care of him and always have and always will. That boy in the mirrored chrome of that medicine cabinet on 119 Clinton Dr. Not the one underneath. The one in the foreground. BUNNY COLVIN is who steals muscle cars and drives them into the ocean.

BUNNY COLVIN is who smashed his heels through the floors when he landed back down from jumping not as high as he could but jumping so as to land as HARD as he could.

BUNNY COLVIN is who arrives when I am completely done talking, I guess you'd say. Which is a place beyond thinking for me, I guess you'd also say? Because COLVIN isn't focused on anything else; he is the purest representation of the Player 2 cowboy who died and died and died every time he spawned, and the Player 1 cowboy who laughed and laughed and laughed every time he pressed the ACTION button on his controller in the syncopated pattern that made it impossible--absolutely and electronically impossible--for him to miss the shot that would always hit its mark as long as he was still in rhythm. BUNNY COLVIN is the guarantor of that protection.
