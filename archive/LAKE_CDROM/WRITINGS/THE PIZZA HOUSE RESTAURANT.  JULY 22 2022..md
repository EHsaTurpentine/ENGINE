I don’t know when I really finished this one; I don’t think it was in the hospital. But the proof is right there, I guess. This was when I was still falling in love but still nevertheless learning how to use the Apple stylus, which has become an important part of my workflows. Having a program like Obsidian at my disposal and synching to every device has unlocked a bunch of things I’ve been lost in the wilderness with for years and for a lot of reasons. One of the last things I did before the wheels fell completely off my second marriage was to backup all of my files to Google drive just in case but moreso for the inevitable. And let’s be completely real about what happens if I’m living in any other way with anyone else when my own inevitabilities ruptured and splattered. I saw the worst parts of her I would ever see those last few years, but we were both just getting warmed up and we were both getting ready to lock all the doors and windows and black everything out between us for good. I knew exactly what I was leaving behind; what I didn’t know was if it would ever be enough to have more than failed at something and still be able to say it was all worth it. I know now that I was always just wasting my time. Which ended up saving my life, in a way.
