14 HOURS: THE CATLEY SIEGE

You rode out of Camp Howard with 59 other fools, chasing a rumor of dead Chinese miners and a killer nobody's named. Now you're pinned on a bald hilltop while Lieutenant Henry Catley — your commanding officer — is somewhere back at the fishing hole, unbothered.

You are one of the sixty — marked by a pulsing cyan ring. The other fifty-nine mill around the hilltop on their own, dodging as best they can (which isn't great). Hold this rock for 14 hours. If your body goes down, you wake up as someone else still standing. Only if all sixty fall is it over. The last two hours are the worst of it.
