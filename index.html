EMIGRANT TRAILS OF SOUTHERN IDAHO

Adventures in the Past — Idaho Cultural Resource Series, Number 1 (1993)

as authored by Daniel J. Hutchison (Bureau of Land Management) and Jerry R. Jones (Idaho State Historical Society).

The purpose of this report was in part to raise public awareness of the cultural resources on public lands — in this case, the physical remains of the emigrant road system that carried Oregon- and California-bound wagon trains across southern Idaho. Emigrant roads closely followed a great number of the Shoshoni, Bannock, and Paiute travel/trade routes, since at least the times of ancient Lake Bonneville...
